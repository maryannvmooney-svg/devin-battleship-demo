⚓ Battleship

San Francisco Bay Edition

Place your ships

Choose a ship, then tap the board to place it.

How to play
  1. Place your fleet. Pick a ship from the list, then click or tap a square on Your fleet. The ship starts on that square and extends right (horizontal) or down (vertical).
  2. Rotate with the Rotate button (or press R). Click a placed ship to pick it up and move it. Ships can't overlap or hang off the board. Random placement sets up the whole fleet for you.
  3. Press Start battle when all five ships are placed: Carrier (5), Battleship (4), Cruiser (3), Submarine (3), Destroyer (2).
  4. Take turns firing. On your turn, click a square on Enemy waters. Orange is a hit, white dot is a miss. You can't fire at the same square twice.
  5. A ship sinks when every square of it is hit. Sink all five enemy ships before the computer sinks yours to win!
